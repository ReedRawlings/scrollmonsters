Bestiary prototypes

Four ways to show the same monsters. All four use the same roster, the same sprites and the same mock save. Open one, then use the DEMO bar at the bottom to switch between a new save, a mid-game save and a complete save.

What the research says

  • 1. Information is the rewardHollow Knight's Hunter's Journal and Monster Hunter World's research levels fill in as you play. New facts feel earned. Hunter's Journal, MH World research
  • 2. Show the gapsDark silhouettes and "N of M" counters pull players to finish a set. An 11 of 12 set motivates more than 0 of 12. Bestiary Codex, Collection sets
  • 3. Seen is not ownedPalworld logs a Pal as a silhouette when you meet it. It fills in only after you capture one. Paldeck guide
  • 4. Portrait first, numbers secondThe Pokédex puts a large sprite next to a short two or three sentence note. Stats come after. Pokédex
  • 5. Show what changes playMonster Hunter lists weak points and drops. The best entries answer "what do I do about this?" for foes and "what do I pair it with?" for allies.
  • 6. Tie it to the player's storyHades' Codex fills in as you meet, talk to and defeat each subject. Personal history makes an entry feel like yours. Hades Codex

The Bestiary Codex and Collection Sets pages blocked direct access in this environment. Those two points come from search summaries only.

The four directions

1 · FIELD GUIDE

The classic dex

A scrolling list on the left. A large animated portrait, radar chart and tabs on the right. It copies the game's current DarkMode style.

Fastest to scanClosest to today's screenLeast surprising
2 · HUNTER'S JOURNAL

Research fills the page

Every section is on the page. Marker bars cover what you have not researched. A rank-up wipes the bars away. The sketch goes from silhouette to pencil to full colour.

Best progression storyCheap to buildBreaks the dark style
3 · CARD BINDER

Collect them all

Every entry is a card in a sleeve. Unmet cards show a card back. Seen cards are ghosts. Mastered cards get foil. Hover tilts a card. Tap one to inspect it and flip it.

Most fun to browseSuits unlock momentsHardest to port
4 · EVOLUTION WEB

The game's own hook

Creatures are stars. Gold arrows are evolution recipes. Teal dashes are party combos. Foes switch to a timeline of when they arrive and who calls whom.

Shows merge and combosFinds loose endsPoor for browsing a list

My suggestion

Mix them. Use 1 Field Guide as the base screen because it is the safest on a phone and the closest to what you ship. Add the research ranks from 2 as the data model, so every locked row shows what is missing. Make the Evolutions tab the Web from 4, because merging is your most unusual feature. Keep the card flip and foil from 3 for the moment a creature unlocks, where your game already has a reveal.
