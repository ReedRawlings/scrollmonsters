BESTIARY CONCEPTS

Four work-in-progress directions for the ScrollMonsters bestiary. Each one uses the game's real art, the NovelMix font and its logical screen sizes.

FRAME
SAVE

Shared rules in all four

REVEAL
Each entry climbs a ladder: ??? → SEEN (faceset silhouette, name, type, wild stats) → CAUGHT (colour art, ratings, attack, dex text, fusions) → MASTERED (records and a field note). Use the SAVE buttons to see each step.
TYPES
Each base creature has one type, taken from its attack. A fusion takes one type from each parent. Example: Owl (WING) + Storm Lizard (STORM) = Tengu (WING/STORM).
DATA
Wild HP, speed and size come from the game code. Fusion HP and party bonuses are the real values. Star ratings, species names, types, dash names and dex text are draft copy.
STYLE
NovelMix at 9 and 18 only. Art at whole-number scale. The red cover is the game's danger red. The device screen uses the DarkMode panel, slot and pill art.
KEYS
ARROWS move, ENTER open or A, ESC back or B. Click the stage first.

Research: what makes a great bestiary

  1. Reveal in steps. Terraria shows more data after 1, 10, 25 and 50 kills. Hollow Knight unlocks notes after enough kills. Monster Hunter shows weak points after more hunts. Here: the SEEN → CAUGHT → MASTERED ladder.
  2. Show the gaps. Numbers and silhouettes tell the player what is still missing. The Ammonomicon shows question marks for locked entries. Here: numbered entries, silhouettes, SEEN and OWN counts.
  3. Make it useful in play. The Monster Hunter field guide shows habitats and weak points. Here: attack, dash, party bonus, fusion recipes and a READY flag.
  4. Give it one voice. In Hollow Knight, each entry is in the Hunter's own words. Here: one narrator, two short sentences per entry, and a field note for mastery.
  5. Make it an object in the world. The Ammonomicon is a book. The Pokédex is a device. Here: binder, handheld and album.
  6. Keep the player's records. The Pokédex keeps size records. Monster Hunter counts hunts and captures. Here: caught, merged, runs and best damage (sample numbers).
  7. Reward completion. In Terraria, 10% of the bestiary opens the Zoologist shop, and more entries add more stock. Proposal: mastery gives a cosmetic, a title or a shiny palette.
  8. Make each creature feel alive. Animated sprites and cries. Here: each entry shows the creature's faceset, the small icons keep the walk cycle, and each creature has a cry.
